What we do

Practical consulting for the technologies behind everyday food.

With 25 years of experience, KIDEX works across wheat and flour production, bakery products, confectionery and pasta technology, helping producers turn experience and innovation into successful projects.

01

Bread & Flour Product Optimization

Refine what's already working

Fine-tuning ingredients, processes and recipes to achieve the highest quality, taste and nutritional value in the final baked goods — improving a product without losing what already makes it work.

  • Ingredient and formulation review
  • Process fine-tuning on the existing line
  • Quality, taste and nutritional improvement
  • Technology, equipment and quality control across milling, baking, confectionery and pasta products
  • Milling technology: wheat quality, cleaning and tempering, milling rolls, milling diagrams, flour packaging and labeling
  • Pasta technology: raw materials, mixing, drying, packaging and labeling
  • Bakery technology: raw materials, mixing, fermentation, baking, cooling, freezing, packaging and labeling
  • Use of enzymes and additives in milling, baking, confectionery and pasta products

New Product Development

Build what's next

Creating and launching new products designed to meet market demands and consumer needs — from early concept through to a formulation ready for production and launch.

  • Concept development against real market demand
  • Recipe and formulation design
  • Support through to production and launch

03

Laboratory Equipment

Measure with confidence

KIDEX has excellent competence and technical skills in the use of laboratory equipment for the quality control of wheat and flour, as well as bakery, confectionery and pasta products.

  • Quality control with KPM Analytics equipment, including Chopin Mixolab, Alveograph and SD Matic
  • Brabender and Promylograph equipment, including Farinograph, Extensograph and Amilograph
  • NIR analyzers from Chopin, Foss, Perten and KPM Analytics / Unity Scientific
  • Expert analysis of laboratory results
  • Consulting during the selection of laboratory equipment for wheat, flour, bakery, confectionery and pasta products
